### `[Major]` IR3-M1: `mutation_history` Save/Load Round-Trip Asymmetry (`serialize_ab_project()` vs. `parse_ab_project()`)
* **Sources:** `egm-implementation-readiness` (`IR3-M1`)
* **Affected Scope:** [flet-antibody-studio.md#L277-L278](file:///Users/fkt/Downloads/PROJECT/Fc-engineering-studio/docs/designs/flet-antibody-studio.md#L277-L278), [flet-antibody-studio.md#L300-L302](file:///Users/fkt/Downloads/PROJECT/Fc-engineering-studio/docs/designs/flet-antibody-studio.md#L300-L302), [flet-antibody-studio.md#L307](file:///Users/fkt/Downloads/PROJECT/Fc-engineering-studio/docs/designs/flet-antibody-studio.md#L307), [flet-antibody-studio.md#L368](file:///Users/fkt/Downloads/PROJECT/Fc-engineering-studio/docs/designs/flet-antibody-studio.md#L368)
* **Issue:** `route_mutations_to_chain()` and `remove_preset_from_chain()` append strings up to `MAX_MUTATIONS = 50` (`1000` characters) to `domain.mutation_history` without entry-length or list-length bounds, and `serialize_ab_project()` applies no caps to `mutation_history`. However, `parse_ab_project()` rejects any `mutation_history` list with `> 50` entries or any entry exceeding `MAX_METADATA_LENGTH` (`200`), causing valid editing sessions to save `.ab` files that fail reload.
* **Concrete Fix:** Cap/truncate formatted `mutation_history` entries to `<= 200` (`MAX_METADATA_LENGTH`) at creation, define a matching list bound (e.g., trim oldest FIFO or enforce uniform bounds across `serialize_ab_project()` and `parse_ab_project()`), and test long / `> 50`-entry `mutation_history` round-trips in `tests/test_exporters.py`.
